- Purpose: make ARTS.md's abstract rendering language concrete by comparing actual combat sprites.

- Distinguish **official statements**, **image observations**, and **project judgments**. Form/value analysis concerns public images, not Mega Crit's internal rules.

## 2. Direction Supported by Official Statements

**Official statement:** Casey Yano describes difficulties conveying pose, grip, and expression through the first game's small humanoid sprites. The sequel strengthens clarity, playfulness, liveliness, and rich color while reducing painterliness. Marlowe Dobbe explains that the stronger cartoon direction in early Silent explorations was not adopted. [October 2024 post](https://www.megacrit.com/news/2024-10-02-neowsletter-issue-3/)

**Project judgment:** this does not prescribe hard cel shading everywhere, exactly three colors per material, or a uniform black outline. Public materials did not establish universal production measurements, head ratios, contour weights, or light angles. Label Realta's numeric choices as project decisions.

## 3. Observations of the Downloaded Combat Frame

These **image observations** concern the [downloaded frame](sts2-style-refs/official-combat.png). The [official URL](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot1_new.png) can change; the web research result and direct download showed different characters. Do not reuse an earlier Ironclad description for this Defect file. A composited screenshot does not establish transparent-source edge behavior or layer structure.

### Silhouette and Pose

- Yellow head, teal shoulder cloth, luminous chest, and yellow limbs form major masses. Silhouette and torso center precede small ornaments.
- Narrow arms/wrists/ankles contrast with broad hands/knees/feet; every part is not uniformly rounded and smooth.
- Gaps between arms/torso and separated legs remain readable. Claw-hand connections can be followed at small scale.
- Health and intent are external UI. Combat numbers or decorative frames do not belong in the character painting.

### Values and Color Planes

- Yellow armor and teal cloth first group into major light/dark areas. Structural knee/arm planes are not subdivided into unrelated small polygons.
- Firm shadow edges coexist with soft curved transitions. Broad cloth has gradual value changes; overlaps and armor segments have clearer edges.
- Not every internal color change is outlined. Values separate planes, with dark lines reserved for structure and overlap.
- Teal cloth, yellow armor, and blue chest remain distinct large color masses. Directional cloth traces and subtle armor brushwork remain; pure vector fills or a blanket ban on microtexture are not requirements.
- Uniform black contours do not dominate the whole silhouette. Some boundaries strengthen locally; others separate by local color/value.

### Density in the Full Screen

- Architectural/plant backgrounds can be complex while combat figures read as simpler contrast masses.
- At displayed sprite size, head direction, arm pose, and leg spread precede small flecks or cloth texture.
- Do not fill apparent empty space in an enlarged sprite. That simplification supports small-scale readability.

## 4. Selection/Event Illustration versus Combat Sprites

**Image observation:** the [downloaded Necrobinder selection screen](sts2-style-refs/official-select.png) gives the character and Osty large occupancy. Pink cloth and violet shadow group broadly; dark face and pale bones contrast sharply. Heavy dark contours occur at Osty's edge and major overlaps, with violet outer rim light. Fine brush/paper texture appears in cloth and bone. The larger art window supports longer folds, hand forms, and ambient lighting. [Official original](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot3_new.png)

**Image observation:** [Neow's event](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot5_new.png) shows broad soft transitions on a rounded teal body and firmer eyes/ornaments. This contradicts an interpretation that StS2 has no gradients.

**Project judgment:** reduce selection-scene ornament density for Realta's combat base. Large color planes must not turn organic faces/bodies into folded-paper shards. Vary transition softness by skin, cloth, leather, and metal.

### Additional Animation Still Observations

**Image observation:** [Silent frame 0](sts2-style-refs/official-silent-anim-frame0.png) from the [official GIF](https://www.megacrit.com/images/silent_anim.gif) shows a hit. Numbers/effects cover part of the head, so it is unsuitable as the final normal-face comparison. Wide stance, flowing cloak silhouette, broad gradual cloth values, and dark fold overlaps remain visible. Leg bandages are broad strips, not individual fibers.

**Image observation:** [Necrobinder frame 0](sts2-style-refs/official-necro-anim-frame0.png) from the [official GIF](https://www.megacrit.com/images/osty_poke3.gif) separates scythe, long neck, arms, and pink robe at small size. Major robe curves, a few folds, fingers, and large joints convey structure. Osty's bones combine broken value planes and round transitions. GIF dithering contributes to the dotted pattern; do not copy it as material texture.

**Project judgment:** label enlargement of small GIF frames in comparison boards. Compare major structure and values rather than translating compression artifacts into high-resolution noise instructions.

### Features Confirmed in Additional Crops

- **[Silent normal-pose crop](sts2-style-refs/silent-combat-crop.png):** pale skull mask, dark inner face, and teal hood separate clearly without hit effects. Broad cloth curves use gradual values; fold ends/overlaps are firmer. Pale bandages, mask, and blade connect the structure without thick black contour everywhere. Low-contrast variation remains inside the hood. [Original GIF](https://www.megacrit.com/images/silent_anim.gif)
- **[Ironclad combat crop](sts2-style-refs/ironclad-combat-crop.png):** golden armor in the co-op frame uses rounded major shoulder planes and bright edges. Arm, glove, waist armor, and knee separate without small ornaments. Large planes and dark connections replace repeated surface blotches. Other figures/swords overlap; this is not a complete isolated sprite. [Original frame](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot2_new.png)
- **[Necrobinder combat crop](sts2-style-refs/necro-combat-crop.png):** long simple neck/ribcage/shoulder and robe forms. Pale bone, dark robe interior, and blue flame read before face detail. Foreground Ironclad/sword occlude parts; do not claim to observe hidden anatomy. [Original frame](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot2_new.png)
- **[Defect combat crop](sts2-style-refs/defect-combat-crop.png):** cloth groups into broad curved folds; yellow armor combines gradual curvature with clear planes. Chest reflections/emission are stronger. Making all materials identical matte three-step surfaces would erase this hierarchy. [Original frame](https://www.megacrit.com/images/steam_screenshot1_new.png)

**Project judgment:** match the reading order rather than merely the presence/absence of lines or texture: silhouette/major values → materials/key overlaps → quiet brushwork/lines. For Realta's bronze pauldrons, use Ironclad's simple plate structure and Defect's broad curvature without copying their armor shapes.

## 5. Concrete Translation for Realta

These are **project judgments** for a costume/weapon absent from the original game, not official rules.

| Part | Recommended treatment | Draft failure to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Face | Broad forehead/cheek light, major hat shadow, simple eyes/mouth conveying direction/confidence | Sparkles, multiple lashes, dense iris rings dominating an anime portrait; angular facial shards |
| Red bob | Silhouette/flow first; a few major locks with limited light planes | Thin lines and spot highlights on every strand |
| Blue hat/coat | Broad blue planes and overlap shadows; folds motivated by bending cloth | Repeated armor-like light/dark triangles or uniform tiny folds |
| Bronze armor | Broad value changes explaining rounded shoulder/chest curvature; selective rim reflection | Unrelated brown patches, rust/scratches everywhere, glossy plastic |
| Collar/fur trim | Continuous ivory mass with a few outer tufts; retain both shoulders and connected neck structure | Each hair outlined until the white edge fragments |
| Wood shaft | Aged medium brown, major values, a few directional grain lines, slight bow | Dense cracks/knots, repeated black lines, protruding nodes, severe zigzags |
| Broom straw | Large fan silhouette split into a few bundles with limited outer separation | Hundreds of fine strands that shimmer or blur when reduced |
| Glaive blade | Broad dark metal and selected bright cutting edge | Chrome/plastic covered in white shine; many ornamental fragments |
| Socket | Preserve bronze socket, two rivets, red ribbon as simple masses | Replacing construction with cloth/leather or adding hardware during material cleanup |

## 6. Comparison Procedure for Subsequent Generation

This is an art-observation sequence, not new tests or acceptance thresholds.

1. Compare an actual official combat figure and candidate at similar displayed heights, not only enlarged.
2. Trace silhouette: distinguish hat, head, both shoulders, gripping hand, shaft, and both legs.
3. Compare fragmentation of costume planes; ask whether they explain major form rather than counting fragments.
4. Avoid uniformly hard or uniformly blurred shadows. Retain curvature on armor/skin and clear overlaps.
5. If broom/fur/wood lines attract attention first at small size, reduce their line density and contrast.
6. Preserve identity/expression. Similarity does not require copying an official mask or skull face.
7. Specify the main observed differences for the next revision. Do not merely repeat "StS2 style" or keep editing rejected candidates.

## 7. Installed-Build Spine Renders (2026-10-24)

Scope differs from sections 1–6: these are renders of the locally installed game (`release_info.json`: v0.107.1, commit `59260271`), not public press material. They are observations only; the rendered PNGs are not redistributed. Regenerate them in a temporary output directory using the procedure below; no saved local render is required.

**Procedure:** `asset-pipeline.ps1 official-poses` ([pipeline section 3](../../.agents/skills/character-asset-pipeline/SKILL.md#3-observation-and-conversion-tools)) loads the game's own `libspine_godot` extension in MegaDot 4.5.1 (OpenGL compatibility renderer), mounts `SlayTheSpire2.pck`, and renders time 0 of `idle_loop` from `res://animations/characters/<c>/<c>_skel_data.tres` and `relaxed_loop` from `res://animations/merchant/<c>/<c>_merchant_skel_data.tres` for Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Necrobinder and Regent. Each frame is rendered on a transparent viewport, converted from the viewport's premultiplied color to straight alpha, then framed to its rendered alpha bounds plus a uniform margin of 5% of the longer side. That framing only trims empty transparent canvas for research use ([ARTS.md 1.3](../../ARTS.md#13-composition-principles-for-all-assets) item 4); nothing is repainted, scaled after capture, or removed. Each skeleton includes its own soft ground-shadow attachment, below alpha 128 in every render; `SubjectFit` measures subjects above that alpha so the shadow does not count as height.

Compared with the press-kit crops, these have no other characters overlapping, no GIF dithering, no scene background, and true alpha, so `compare -CompareMode SubjectFit` can equalize subject heights.

**Measured values** (opaque pixels, alpha > 200; luminance `0.2126R + 0.7152G + 0.0722B`; observations, not thresholds):

|---|---|---|---|
| Ironclad | 202 | 0.4% | 0.54 |

**Image observations** at equal subject height:

- **Paint handling in official monsters/masked heroes vs. project character sprites:** the five official Spine renders use matte, opaque brushstrokes and dungeon-compressed values suited to masked, hooded, or non-human designs (Ironclad, Silent, Defect, Necrobinder, Regent). However, applying heavy dry-brush gouache texture, muted pewter/ash value compression, or squat 6-head proportions directly to unmasked human character sprites makes them look clunky and rough. Use these renders to judge **grounded stance, silhouette separation, negative space, and display-size readability**, while keeping clean cel-plus-soft-form 2D shading, crisp facial linework, and sleek canonical proportions from [ARTS.md section 2.1](../../ARTS.md#21-target-direction).
- **Material values in official Spine renders:** in the five official skeletons, light surfaces sit in an upper-middle band (max luminance 202–245). For mod characters with canonical white cloth or silver hair (such as Althea), preserve clean warm ivory/cream cloth and bright silver-white hair without muddy gray/pewter tinting, while still avoiding flat `#FFFFFF` blowouts that erase internal folds.
- **Proportions:** official skeletons vary widely by character concept (crouched masked rogues, floating liches, bulky armor). Do not force sleek human knight/mage designs into squat 6–6.5-head proportions or oversized boots/gauntlets; preserve the character's canonical 7–7.5-head silhouette and judge readability through clear limb/weapon spacing at `240 × 360`.
- **Trim and ornament:** consolidate micro-noise into clean, readable color bands and sharp emblem shapes that survive downscaling.

## 8. Limitations

- Public sources do not establish internal brushes, layer structure, texture resolution, rigs, exact color counts, or outline thickness.
- Official GIFs are development-era combat material, not proof of identical frames in all released versions.
- The web tool did not directly play GIFs. Record still-form observations separately from animation-timing observations.
- Combat frames are not alpha-separated sprites; surrounding color can affect perceived edges.
- Canonical approval and downstream-production approval remain with the user. Research does not replace approval or imply runtime application.
