## Delivery and review

The canonical PNG is 1024 × 1536 RGBA, matching Althea's delivery dimensions and the 2:3 aspect ratio shared with Realta's 1152 × 1728 standing base. The approved square v7 source is 1024 × 1024, previously downscaled from the complete 1254 × 1254 generated canvas. For the explicitly authorized format conversion, retain source rectangle (x234, y29, width598, height897), then use proportional Fit to 1024 × 1536 (scale 1024/598). Every removed pixel has alpha zero; the retained pixels were unchanged before bicubic resampling. [Asset status](assets.md) owns preservation evidence, margins and runtime coverage.

The portrait output was opened at full size and in 240 × 360 light/dark comparisons with Althea and Realta using IndividualFit. A SubjectFit comparison against the square v7 source confirmed preserved silhouette, contacts and colors at equivalent subject size. SubjectFit includes feathers and raised weapons in its height calculation; these observations do not establish actual in-game body scale. Future review checks drift from this approved base and the requested use case, rather than reopening the selected rendering preference.
