## Basic Strike candidate

Production brief: a compact medieval crossbow in three-quarter closeup, with one bolt just leaving its central rail toward upper-right. A wooden stock, paired transverse steel bow limbs, connected string, trigger housing and a few brass fittings explain the mechanism. Align the bolt with the stock's axis. Use one bright ivory projectile trail, broad angular cel-shaded planes and a crimson/burgundy background; omit people, hands, scenery, lettering and card UI. Wood, steel and brass are provisional choices for this candidate.

The built-in image generator used native `StrikeIronclad`/`StrikeSilent` portraits for card style and composition only. At 250 x 190 the bow limbs, stock, string and departing bolt remain identifiable. This is a review candidate, not an approved canonical weapon design.

### Dragon's Waymark geometry refinement

The user found the first ring squashed and then rejected the raised gemstone mount in the second refinement. The current third candidate is a low-profile inset-stone ring: a smaller blue gem sits directly inside the widened top band, with a thin rim and no cylindrical pedestal or tall triangular shoulders. The hoop forms one smooth continuous contour. Silver, blue gemstone, restrained antique wear, transparency and the existing game-icon rendering remain invariant. The built-in edit used the second ring as its selected target and explicitly removed the protruding mount. At 94 and 48 px the inset sapphire remains legible while the ring outline reads as one coherent hoop. The candidate exports now use this third refinement.

The fourth user-requested refinement enlarges only the sapphire face and gives it fuller facets while retaining the third candidate's low inset mounting and continuous hoop. No elevated pedestal returns. Both 94 px before/after and 48 px previews were opened; the larger blue area remains readable without dominating the ring silhouette. This fourth refinement is the current candidate.
