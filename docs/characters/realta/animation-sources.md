# Realta combat and merchant animation sources

The combat and merchant wrappers share the rig cut from the 1152×1728 [base painting](base.png). The shipped representation uses Godot `Skeleton2D`, weighted `Polygon2D` attachments and a native `AnimationPlayer`. The original rig-c coordinates use the top-left of the painting as the origin, +X right and +Y down. Paired bone, slot, and attachment suffixes (`_r`/`_l`) follow the character's anatomical perspective (`_r` = right/near side, `_l` = left/far side; see [paired limb and part naming](/docs/conventions/characters/naming.md#paired-limb-and-part-naming)), while broom ribbons use `ribbon_long` and `ribbon_short`. See the [scene mapping](animation-assets.md#current-scene-and-property-connections).

## Inputs and ownership

| Source | Owns |
|---|---|
| [layout.json](animation/rigc/layout.json), [atlas.txt](animation/rigc/atlas.txt), and [realta_skeleton.png](/Sts2WD/animations/realta/combat/realta_skeleton.png) | Source canvas, registered bone pivots, part rectangles, state names, texture resolution and final part pixels. [unpack_atlas.py](animation/rigc/authoring/unpack_atlas.py) unpacks all 58 part PNGs losslessly from `realta_skeleton.png` during builds. Keep `textureScale: 3` on the eyes and mouth. |
| [author_rig.py](animation/rigc/authoring/author_rig.py) | Bone hierarchy, setup slots, regions, weighted meshes, separate blade/shaft registration, spell and attack-thrust FX attachments and the attachment choices used by each clip (mirroring `hand_l.png` on `hand_r` for the death open state, using ulnar-side `hand_r-ground.png` for the palm-down death floor state, and reusing `hand_r.png` on `hand_l` for the two-handed attack grip). |
| [author_motion.py](animation/rigc/authoring/author_motion.py) | The seven poses and timelines, IK targets, hand switches, per-clip draw order, face states, uniform revival fade and cues. [Kinematics](animation/rigc/authoring/kinematics.py) and [sampling](animation/rigc/authoring/sampling.py) resolve the poses to linear transform keys. |
| [garment.py](animation/rigc/authoring/garment.py) and [wrist_seams.py](animation/rigc/authoring/wrist_seams.py) | Shared weights and supports for the skirt, and the distal bracer supports used by the two-handed attack. |
| [build.ps1](animation/rigc/build.ps1) and [bake_godot.py](animation/rigc/authoring/bake_godot.py) | Compile all seven motions into a single-pass rig-c skeleton and 2048×2048 RGBA parts-only sprite sheet (`realta_skeleton.png`), then bake the Godot bones, attachments, animation tracks and grouped revival fade (`Visuals:group_alpha`). |
| [import_game_rig.py](/scripts/realta/import_game_rig.py) and [realta_draw_order.gd](/Sts2WD/animations/realta/combat/realta_draw_order.gd) | Bind the shared body and clip resources to the combat and merchant players, derive `clips/revive_merchant.tres` directly from the live skeleton poses, and toggle Godot's `CANVAS_GROUP_MODE_TRANSPARENT` only while `Visuals:group_alpha` is translucent. |

The setup and attack use the same source drawing of the glaive. The final rig has 39 bones and 41 slots, including the two cuff supports, garment support, three cast sigil layers and two attack thrust FX layers (`thrust_streak`, `thrust_flash`), with no full-body composite sprites. A rebuild derives the runtime images and transforms from these inputs. [Rebuild and package playback](animation/reproduction.md)

## Clips and dispatch

| Clip | Duration | Authored action |
|---|---:|---|
| `idle_loop` | 2.60 s | Upright breathing loop. Both eyes blink from 1.84 to 1.975 s with the neutral mouth. |
| `attack` | 1.05 s | Lower the glaive in front of the body, catch the wood with the supporting hand, coil back while chambering the lead leg, then snap forward with a piercing wind streak (`thrust_streak`) and additive tip flash (`thrust_flash`). The `attack_hit` cue and full extension land at 0.54 s with a brief impact hold before recovery. |
| `hurt` | 0.80 s | Torso and pelvis recoil, the knees yield and the hurt face appears. |
| `cast` | 1.30 s | Gather low and extend the anatomical left arm behind the torso. The `hand_l-casting` glove extends the index and middle fingers, with the ring and little fingers curled toward the palm. The three-layer sigil emits on `cast_release` at 0.25 s. |
| `relaxed_loop` | 8.00 s | Crouch, prop the chin on the back of the anatomical right glove (`hand_r-back`) and hum. Both legs tuck into the garment and the broom-glaive hovers beside the body. |
| `die` | 2.20 s | Release the broom on `weapon_release` at 0.12 s, catch briefly on the knees, lose the hat and collapse forward. By 1.66 s the cheek, body and palm have reached the floor. The hair and clothing finish settling through 2.20 s. |
| `revive` | 0.40 s | Fade the settled death pose out, switch the pose at 0.20 s while hidden, and fade in the standing combat pose or the crouching merchant pose. |

Non-Spine animation dispatch, alias routing, return clips, and model timing constants live in [BaseLib dispatch](animation-api.md) and [Realta.cs](/Sts2WDCode/Characters/Realta/Realta.cs).

## Completed parts and overlap ownership

- **Body and arms.** `pauldron_r`, `upper_arm_r`, `forearm_r`, and `hand_r` form the anatomical right arm (near/camera side, holding the broom-glaive). The shoulder and elbow on this near arm pass in front of the breastplate during the thrust. The opposite anatomical left arm (`pauldron_l`, `upper_arm_l`, `forearm_l`, `hand_l`) stays behind the body; at 0.31 s its glove switches to the front grip (reusing `hand_r.png`) and draws over the shaft until 0.695 s. The two attack bracer meshes (`cuff_r`, `cuff_l`) move their distal openings into the thick part of each glove. The weapon contacts remain on the original wood.
- **Weapon.** At 0.145 s the attack replaces the one-piece weapon with the original shaft and a rigidly mirrored copy of the painted blade and socket, registered to the shaft axis. Both palms and the broom ribbons (`ribbon_long`, `ribbon_short`) stay on their existing wood positions. The original orientation returns at 0.815 s. Released during death, the broom lies horizontally with bristles to the left. The hat settles separately ahead of the face.
- **Skirt and legs.** `coat_back` owns the blue lining behind both legs. The two cream-trimmed front panels (`coat_r`, `coat_l` with `skirt_hem_r`, `skirt_hem_l`) cover the legs (`thigh_r`/`shin_r`/`foot_r`/`boot_r`, `thigh_l`/`shin_l`/`foot_l`/`boot_l`) on either side of the slit. Front and back share the same waist and side-seam deformation. The side lifts over the advancing knee, and the hem falls back toward the boot. Rounded thigh, knee and ankle overlaps supply the hidden ends.
- **Face.** The head (with `hair_r`, `hair_l`) owns the cheek, nose and hairline. Slots `eye_r` (right/near eye) and `eye_l` (left/far eye) own their corresponding eyebrow, eyelid and lashes. `mouth` owns the mouth. The three slots provide neutral, closed, hurt, attack, pondering and fallen states at three texels per base pixel. Each state's rectangle is explicitly registered in the layout. [Expression mapping](animation-verification.md#combat-expressions)
- **Death and revival.** The death uses the same articulated body, hat, weapon and skirt as the standing rig. The right fist (`hand_r`) opens after release by mirroring and reusing `hand_l.png`, rolls to follow the fall and finishes with the palm on the floor. The fallen face has shut eyes, tense brows and a parted mouth. Both combat and merchant `revive` clips drive the live articulated skeleton directly, collapsing the uniform body fade onto `Visuals:group_alpha` so `realta_draw_order.gd` enables Godot's `CANVAS_GROUP_MODE_TRANSPARENT` only while translucent—preserving armor and coat overlaps without pre-rendered full-body sprites.
