# Althea Relic Deliveries

Each slug below has a 94 x 94 `<slug>.png`, a 94 x 94 `<slug>_outline.png`, and a 256 x 256 `big/<slug>.png` under `Sts2WD/images/relics/`. Fit the whole composition to 256 x 256 for the large icon and to 90 x 90 for the small icon. Center the small icon in the 94 x 94 canvas and derive its white outline by dilating the alpha by 2 px.
