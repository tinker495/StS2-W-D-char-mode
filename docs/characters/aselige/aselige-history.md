# Aselige Artwork History

## Ancient Event and Dialogue

The user chose the close, spread-wing composition to establish Aselige's scale. The applied painting keeps that framing and places the dragon's foreclaw on a moss-covered stump in an ancient forest. The continuous wing membranes and relaxed foreclaw define the final posture. The separate dialogue head matches the forest painting's branching chestnut horns, blue irises, and green scales in a compact silhouette for the native 85 px speaker icon. [Event art](event-art.md) and [dialogue head art](ui-art.md) own the approved appearance.
