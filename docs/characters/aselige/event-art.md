# Aselige Event Art

The user approved the refined forest dragon and matching dialogue head for the Ancient implementation. The 1875 x 839 forest painting in [aselige.png](/Sts2WD/images/ancients/aselige.png) is the canonical identity reference and is used unchanged in the Ancient event.

Aselige is a majestic, slender serpentine green dragon with an elegant S-curving neck, dark-green scales, olive highlights, blue irises and exactly one pair of chestnut-brown horns branching like tree limbs. The spread wings may extend beyond the painted canvas to convey scale. Preserve the full image during delivery. Wing membranes are continuous, and a relaxed foreclaw rests on a huge mossy stump in an ancient forest.

The event texture fills the viewport with proportional Cover (`KeepAspectCovered`), preserving vertical framing and the central dragon and stump composition while allowing outer wingtip margins to extend beyond narrower aspect ratios such as 16:9. No UI, text or frame is painted into it. Follow [ARTS.md](/ARTS.md) for shared rendering and composition rules.

See [event assets](event-assets.md) for delivery and [dialogue head art](ui-art.md) for the separate miniature composition.
